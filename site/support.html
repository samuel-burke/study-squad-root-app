Support

Stuck, found a bug, or want to suggest something? Reach out any way you like.

  • Email: hello@samuelburke.dev
  • Root: message @5amuel
  • In the app: open Room settings and tap Send feedback. Notes sent this way are anonymous, so include a way to reach you if you'd like a reply.
  • Bugs and ideas: open an issue on GitHub. Please don't put personal details in a public issue.

Your data

To delete your data, use the delete option in the app, or email us if you can't reach the app. See the privacy policy for details.
